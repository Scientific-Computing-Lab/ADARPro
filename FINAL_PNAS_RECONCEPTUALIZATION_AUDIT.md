# Final adversarial PNAS-style audit

Date: 2026-09-09

## Editorial verdict

The revised manuscript now presents a biological information map rather than a
model-launch paper. Its opening question is what current measurements can
recover about endogenous A-to-I editing across duplex suitability, substrate
propensity, cellular state, and target selection. ADARPro appears late as the
frozen operational synthesis and validation of conclusions established during
development.

A skeptical editor can now identify a field-level contribution independent of
the benchmark gain: sequence and predicted structure carry substantial but
non-universal information; substrate organization marks a separate propensity
layer; existing bulk annotations fail to resolve the corresponding cellular
state; target/helix-partner alignment recovers local selectivity; and multiple
richer formulations approach a conditional empirical plateau.

## Adversarial questions

### Is this still “another somewhat better predictor”?

No. The title omits the model name, the Significance Statement and Abstract
lead with the biological gap, and the Results progress by scientific question.
Figures 1--3 establish the hierarchy, baseline scope, transfer limits, and
unresolved context before the enabling architecture appears. The canonical
benchmark improvement is presented as held-out validation of the map.

### Are predictive results being passed off as mechanisms?

No. The manuscript explicitly distinguishes prediction from explanation.
Target alignment is described as localization of predictive information, pair
geometry as consistent with realization or engagement, and residual error as a
mixture that cannot be apportioned among missing state, measurement error,
finite data, representation, and stochasticity. Causal verbs are avoided where
there is no perturbational evidence.

### Are negative results biologically interpretable?

Yes, within stated bounds. RBP, transcript, repeat, and chromatin results are
restricted to the tested assays and resolutions. Peak absence is not treated as
biological absence, and failure of bulk context is used to motivate matched
cell-, donor-, transcript-, and state-resolved measurement rather than to reject
the underlying biology.

### Is the claimed ceiling too broad?

No. “Ceiling” is expressed as an empirical boundary for the tested
measurements, representations, effective sample size, and benchmark. The paper
does not claim a universal upper bound on A-to-I editing prediction. Cross-
lineage transfer is explicitly confounded by lineage, substrate class, assay,
and data generation.

### Does the figure sequence carry the thesis without the text?

Yes. Figure 1 defines the multiscale information hierarchy and evaluation
logic. Figure 2 shows substantial within-context signal and structured transfer
failure. Figure 3 places positive substrate-level evidence beside the current
contextual measurement gap. Figure 4 shows that substrate-aware learning and
target alignment recover orthogonal information. Figure 5 validates those
principles and ends with captured, partially resolved, and missing information.

### Do the figures meet a publication-level biological visual standard?

Yes after a second redesign pass. Recent PNAS and Nature Communications RNA,
genome-mapping, and systems-atlas figures were inspected at full-panel scale,
and six biology-oriented PaperBanana references guided hierarchy and density.
Each main figure now combines information-bearing vector plots with a curated
biological orientation layer. Fifteen GPT Image 1.5 candidates were generated
across five nonquantitative asset families; clipped, overly decorative, or
scientifically ambiguous candidates were rejected. The five retained assets
are embedded beneath editable labels, arrows, legends, and quantitative marks.
The final 9-page PDF was rasterized and inspected page by page at 110 dpi and
the individual figures were inspected at their native 450-dpi PNG size.

## Remaining author-controlled items

Author names, affiliations, contributions, competing-interest disclosure,
corresponding-author details, acknowledgments, and funding remain explicit
placeholders. These cannot be inferred from the evidence repository and must be
completed before submission.

## Final recommendation

The manuscript is ready for scientific-author review under the revised thesis.
That review should concentrate on whether the proposed information hierarchy
matches the authors' intended biological interpretation and whether the
bounded language around substrate realization is acceptable. It should not
revert the article to a model-first architecture narrative.
