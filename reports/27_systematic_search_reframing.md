# Systematic-search reframing

## Scientific thesis

A controlled search across the major biological information scales accessible
to the benchmark locates reproducible signal at whole-substrate and
target-local scales, shows that current bulk context measurements do not
resolve the remaining site-level variation, and identifies finer matched
biological measurement rather than generic model sophistication alone as the
next limiting resource.

## Verified search axes

The retained campaign maps to three biological scales: intrinsic
whole-duplex/substrate suitability, transcript or cellular realization, and
target-local selection within a duplex. Dependence among sites from the same
substrate is treated separately as a statistical-learning problem. A secondary
model-sufficiency axis groups the computational search into four classes:
supervision richness, cross-context sharing or multitask learning,
representation sophistication, and model capacity or integration.

The branch-level coverage table records the candidate information, comparator,
outcome class, evidence regime, and disposition. Unsupported results are
restricted to the tested formulation. Limited results are separated from
negative results, and experiments requiring donor-, cell-, transcript-, or
in-vivo-structure-matched measurements are marked deferred rather than
negative. Superseded directions remain visible.

## Main-text changes

- The title, Significance Statement, Abstract, and Introduction now define the
  biological scale decomposition before describing individual experiments.
- Results follow the search topology rather than campaign chronology.
- The model-sufficiency section synthesizes the four modeling classes instead
  of listing architectures. It concludes conditionally that pair-first
  learning and correctly centered target-local representation produced the
  clearest gains under the tested regime, whereas generic increases in
  supervision richness, transfer, pretrained representation, capacity, and
  connected integration were limited, neutral, or adverse.
- The final predictor is presented as a frozen synthesis test of supported
  principles, not as the manuscript's organizing contribution.
- The Discussion distinguishes recoverable information, unresolved contextual
  variation, and measurements unavailable at the required biological scale.

## Evidence boundary

No new experimental result was generated for this reframing. Development OOF
comparisons continue to support component attribution, while the untouched
canonical test validates only the frozen synthesis on unseen pairs within the
benchmark. The conclusion about diminishing returns applies to the tested
measurements, representations, effective sample size, and evaluation regime;
it is not a universal limit on AI or future architectures.
